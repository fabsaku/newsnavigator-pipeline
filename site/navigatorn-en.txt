Independent Russian newspaper Novaya Gazeta shuts down
The independent Russian newspaper Novaya Gazeta is closing, its editors say. The Straits Times and The Hindu attribute the closure to state and Kremlin pressure, and Meduza reports that journalists said the paper was to be evicted from its Moscow office the day before.
How the world sees it: Singapore's The Straits Times and India's The Hindu headline that the paper is closing under pressure, citing state pressure and Kremlin pressure respectively. The UK's BBC highlights the founder's statement that authorities have stifled the paper. Meduza (Latvia) highlights whether the paper can rise again and the editor's view of what comes next. Sweden's SVT only reports that the editorial team is announcing the closure.

One dead and six injured in shooting at Spirit Halloween store in Fairfield, California
Police said one person was killed and six injured when a gunman opened fire at a Spirit Halloween store in a mall in Fairfield, California, on Friday evening. According to Dawn, investigators had not yet identified the person responsible.
How the world sees it: The US source New York Post highlights that the FBI has been deployed. Colombian El Tiempo highlights that the suspect is at large and that three of the injured are in critical condition. Pakistani Dawn highlights police statements and that the shooting occurred shortly before 8:30pm local time. Singaporean CNA highlights that the shooting took place hours before Halloween, while The Straits Times highlights that at least three people are in critical condition. Indian Hindustan Times highlights that at least seven people were shot and that the victims were found in three locations.
