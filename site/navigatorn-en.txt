Israel launches ground operation in central Gaza
19 November 2026 at 03:07 UTC
Israel's military has launched a ground operation in central Gaza, according to several outlets, with tanks crossing the so-called yellow line. The Japan Times reports that the body overseeing the truce says both Israel and Hamas have not met their commitments.
How the world sees it: The Guardian and The New York Times highlight tanks crossing the yellow line and the ceasefire line, respectively. Middle East Eye and the BBC highlight the Israeli military's stated aim of destroying a tunnel. The Japan Times, Hindustan Times and Deutsche Welle highlight people fleeing or being urged to avoid the area. Russian state-controlled RT instead highlights a concurrent visit by the top US military commander for the Middle East to Israel.
