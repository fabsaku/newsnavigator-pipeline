European countries expect to join new Ukraine talks, says Merz
20 November 2026 at 01:10 UTC
German Chancellor Friedrich Merz says European leaders could join new Ukraine talks soon, according to Deutsche Welle. At a meeting in Berlin, European allies of Ukraine say they have a united negotiating position, BBC reports.
How the world sees it: British BBC highlights European unity ahead of talks with Russia. German Deutsche Welle highlights Merz's statement that European leaders could take part on neutral ground, and that the UK and EU countries want a seat at the table. Singapore's The Straits Times and India's Hindustan Times highlight that Europe expects to join talks soon, and The Straits Times names the United Arab Emirates as a possible location. Colombian El Tiempo highlights in its headline a French proposal on Ukraine's path to NATO and a German call for peace in Europe. The UAE's The National, a state-controlled medium, highlights a US pledge of cooperation with Europe on Ukraine-Russia talks.

Israel launches ground operation in central Gaza
19 November 2026 at 03:07 UTC
Israel's military has launched a ground operation in central Gaza, according to several outlets, with tanks crossing the so-called yellow line. The Japan Times reports that the body overseeing the truce says both Israel and Hamas have not met their commitments.
How the world sees it: The Guardian and The New York Times highlight tanks crossing the yellow line and the ceasefire line, respectively. Middle East Eye and the BBC highlight the Israeli military's stated aim of destroying a tunnel. The Japan Times, Hindustan Times and Deutsche Welle highlight people fleeing or being urged to avoid the area. Russian state-controlled RT instead highlights a concurrent visit by the top US military commander for the Middle East to Israel.
