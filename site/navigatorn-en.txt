Democrats take control of US Congress in midterm elections
Democrats took control of both the House of Representatives and the Senate in the US midterm elections, according to several sources. The Wall Street Journal reports that the party also won a majority of governorships.
How the world sees it: American sources mainly focus on what the result means for Trump and Congress, for example Fox News on Senate Republicans and The New York Times on Congress's ability to check the president. CGTN (state-controlled, China) highlights voters rejecting Republicans in historically red states, and The National (state-controlled, United Arab Emirates) highlights the Democrats' 'blue tsunami' and the setback for Trump. Focus Taiwan (Taiwan) highlights that Taiwan continues to enjoy support in the US Congress. Dawn (Pakistan) describes the result as a nationwide rejection of Trump.
US: blue and red: Media rated left or lean left by AllSides: Vox (AllSides: left) has a headline highlighting how Democrats won the Deep South, a regional angle. The New York Times (AllSides: lean left) has a headline highlighting that Congress is set to reassert power to check Trump. Media rated right by AllSides: Fox News (AllSides: right) has a headline saying Senate Republicans will not stand idly by, and its ingress quotes Senate Majority Leader John Thune on 'Democrat extremism'. Media rated center by AllSides: The Hill (AllSides: center) has a headline on demographic shifts behind the Democrats' victory, and The Wall Street Journal (AllSides: center) describes the result as a broad Democratic win in Congress and in governorships.

Abdul El-Sayed wins Michigan Senate race, becoming the first Muslim elected to the US Senate
Democrat Abdul El-Sayed won Michigan's Senate race over Republican Mike Rogers, according to AP and US networks, becoming the first Muslim elected to the US Senate. According to Reuters, the US president repeatedly called him a jihadist during the campaign.
How the world sees it: The sources differ in what they highlight. Axios says the win shows how an openly progressive candidate can win in a battleground state. Al-Monitor, which republishes Reuters, and Singapore's The Straits Times highlight attacks on El-Sayed during the campaign, and Al-Monitor reports that the president called him a jihadist. Hindustan Times (India) highlights that his opponent Mike Rogers was backed by Trump. Daily Maverick (South Africa) highlights the seat's role in the Democrats' fight for Senate control. Dawn (Pakistan) highlights El-Sayed's victory speech. The Hindu (India) asks who El-Sayed is. El Tiempo (Colombia) places the election in a series of new faces in Congress. The National (UAE), a state-controlled outlet, highlights that he becomes the first Muslim elected to the Senate.
