All 69 victims of Spanish train crash identified
Spanish authorities have identified all 69 people killed in a high-speed train collision in Urda, central Spain, on 28 October, according to several outlets. Two Japanese nationals are among the dead, according to The Straits Times and The Japan Times.
How the world sees it: Sources from different countries highlight different aspects of the event. Japanese and Singaporean outlets highlight that two Japanese nationals are among the dead. Mexico News Daily highlights a Mexican-born man living in Oklahoma City who was among the victims. Chinese state media (CGTN) highlight that 29 injured people remain hospitalised. NRK highlights the number of foreign nationals among those identified.

Russia bombards Ukraine's power grid ahead of Abu Dhabi peace talks
Russia struck Ukraine's energy infrastructure with missiles and drones, according to Reuters, a week before US-brokered talks. Witkoff says the US, Russia and Ukraine will hold talks in Abu Dhabi on 10 and 11 November.
How the world sees it: The headlines highlight different parts of the news. Russian state media (RT) highlights Witkoff announcing the next round of negotiations. Emirati state media (The National) highlights the US saying the Abu Dhabi talks will take place this month. Chinese state media (CGTN) highlights the US bringing together delegations from Ukraine and Russia to discuss ending the conflict, post-conflict security agreements and de-escalation. The Kyiv Independent highlights the European Commission hosting talks on Ukraine in Brussels on 4 November, and Euractiv highlights talks between the US, Europe and NATO in Brussels. The Japan Times and Al-Monitor highlight Russia's attacks on Ukraine's power grid and Merz urging Moscow to engage.
US: blue and red: Media that AllSides rates as leaning left (Axios) and as right (Fox News) highlight different things. The Axios headline says the US, Russia and Ukraine will resume peace talks, and its summary notes that diplomacy was suspended in February due to the war in Iran. Axios cites US officials saying the meeting results from talks between Trump's envoys. The Fox News headline says the US will mediate upcoming talks between Ukraine and Russia, and its summary says the talks focus on security arrangements and de-escalation. Fox News reports Witkoff's announcement.
