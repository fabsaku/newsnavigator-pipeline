Brazil's electoral authority: rules were followed in presidential election
Brazil's electoral authority says, according to DR, that all rules were followed in the presidential election. Dagens Nyheter reports that the authority rejects election fraud accusations made by Bolsonaro.
How the world sees it: Danish DR's headline highlights the electoral authority's statement that all rules were followed. Swedish Dagens Nyheter's headline highlights the authority dismissing Bolsonaro's election fraud accusations.

Christine Lagarde to step down as ECB president early, effective 7 February 2027
The European Central Bank (ECB) announced on Thursday that Christine Lagarde will leave her post as president on 7 February 2027, almost nine months before her term ends in October. According to European Council President Antonio Costa, the successor search has begun.
How the world sees it: Some Asian sources (South China Morning Post, Japan Times and Rappler) highlight the economic and political challenges facing Europe, including inflation risks, rising bond yields and the election campaign in France. The Straits Times instead highlights Lagarde's career and her way of leading the ECB. The Hindu highlights the resignation itself. European and Latin American sources (Euractiv, Deutsche Welle, Sveriges Radio Ekot, SVT and El País) mainly highlight the announcement, its timing and the succession question. The state-controlled outlet The National, from the United Arab Emirates, headlines only that Lagarde resigns after months of speculation.
