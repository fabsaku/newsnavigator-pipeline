Five European countries plan return hubs for rejected asylum seekers in Rwanda
17 November 2026 at 13:08 UTC
Five European countries, Austria, Denmark, Germany, Greece and the Netherlands, plan to send rejected asylum seekers to a return hub in Rwanda. According to Premium Times, an initial pilot is expected to involve about 100 people, and human rights groups have criticised the plans.
How the world sees it: The headlines highlight different aspects. African sources highlight Rwanda's role: AllAfrica, carrying text from New Times (Rwanda), writes about a partnership, and Premium Times (Nigeria) writes about a pilot project. European sources, including Deutsche Welle and The Guardian, mainly highlight the plan itself and criticism from human rights groups. The Japan Times (Japan) highlights protests from human rights groups and a warning from the UN refugee agency UNHCR. The National, a state-controlled outlet in the United Arab Emirates, headlines the plan as a scheme to be announced.

The Swedish outlet Breakit highlights that AI agents attacked Hugging Face. The Danish outlet DR mentions only the lawsuit itself.
