Five EU countries plan return hub in Rwanda for rejected asylum seekers
18 November 2026 at 01:09 UTC
According to several outlets, five EU countries, including Germany, Denmark, Austria, Greece and the Netherlands, plan a return hub in Rwanda for rejected asylum seekers. The UN human rights chief and Human Rights Watch express concern.
How the world sees it: The sources highlight different aspects of the story. El Tiempo (Colombia) highlights reactions at the UN and in Europe, The Straits Times (Singapore) highlights the UN human rights chief, Deutsche Welle (Germany) highlights criticism from human rights groups, and The Japan Times (Japan) highlights protests and a warning from UNHCR. The East African (Kenya) and AllAfrica highlight the plan and the partnership itself, and Dagens Nyheter (Sweden) highlights that earlier attempts have failed.
