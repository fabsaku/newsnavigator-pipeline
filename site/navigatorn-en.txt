Trump threatens to block or tax US firms building AI data centres abroad
21 November 2026 at 01:07 UTC
Trump said on Friday that US companies building AI data centres overseas could be blocked or face a very high tax. According to the BBC, he gave few details about the proposal, and others in his team reportedly heard about it for the first time.
How the world sees it: The Spanish outlet El País highlights that the president encouraged local communities to accept data centres despite growing opposition to the facilities. The Hong Kong-based South China Morning Post highlights a divide between the president's view that expansion is critical to the AI race and a US public that is sceptical of its benefits.

Merz: European leaders could join new Ukraine talks soon
20 November 2026 at 09:05 UTC
German Chancellor Friedrich Merz says European leaders could join new Ukraine talks soon. The United Arab Emirates is named as a possible location, according to The Straits Times.
How the world sees it: German (Deutsche Welle), Singaporean (The Straits Times) and Indian (Hindustan Times) sources mainly highlight Merz's statement that Europe is expected to join new talks. British BBC highlights that European allies say they have a united negotiating position and are ready for talks with Russia. The National, from the United Arab Emirates and identified in the source material as a state-controlled medium, highlights that the US pledges cooperation with Europe on the talks.
