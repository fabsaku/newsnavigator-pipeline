Flavio Bolsonaro leaves Brazil for US after election defeat
Flavio Bolsonaro has left Brazil for the US after losing the presidential election to Luiz Inácio Lula da Silva, according to several sources. He says he is Brazil's rightful leader, and his family is reported to have travelled with him.
How the world sees it: European and Asian sources (Deutsche Welle, Hindustan Times, the Straits Times, The Hindu and DR) mainly highlight Bolsonaro's statements about his position and that he flew to Florida. Russian state-controlled RT highlights investigations into alleged financial crimes. The state-controlled outlet The National from the United Arab Emirates highlights that Bolsonaro did not hand in his passport. Al Jazeera from Qatar highlights that he is an ally of Donald Trump, and the Buenos Aires Times from Argentina highlights reports that he is seeking political asylum.
US: blue and red: The Washington Examiner, which AllSides rates as leaning right, highlights in its headline that Bolsonaro declared himself the true elected president and that his departure had the blessing of the White House. It describes his landing in Miami and his leaving his home. NPR, which AllSides rates as leaning left, highlights that Bolsonaro's party accepts Lula's victory while Bolsonaro will not concede. NPR also highlights that President Trump calls the vote rigged and writes that he offers no evidence for this. Both headlines cover Bolsonaro's position but with different focus: one on his claims of leadership and the White House, the other on the positions of his party and of Trump.
