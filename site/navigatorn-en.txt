COP31 climate summit in Antalya runs into overtime amid dispute over fossil fuels
22 November 2026 at 03:07 UTC
The COP31 climate summit in Antalya, Turkey, has gone into overtime. Several sources report disagreement over how to handle fossil fuels, and the summit presidency says, according to Reuters, that it hopes to reach consensus on a new package.
How the world sees it: British and German sources highlight the deadlock and division between countries. Al-Monitor (Reuters) and The Straits Times highlight that the summit presidency is nearing a final deal and that delegations have a deadline for objections. DR Nyheder highlights the number of countries refusing to give up the fight against fossil fuels. Rappler (Philippines) highlights the risk of failure. The National (United Arab Emirates, state-controlled media) highlights the dispute over the final deal, and CGTN (China, state-controlled media) highlights the dispute over fossil fuels and the extension of talks.
