Polish president calls crisis meeting after clash at court
Polish President Karol Nawrocki calls a crisis meeting after unrest at the constitutional court, according to Swedish Radio's Ekot. According to Ekot, the government tried on Friday to remove the court's head, and thousands of demonstrators gathered outside the building.
How the world sees it: Russian state-controlled media (RT) highlight that the president entered a besieged court and that the Constitutional Tribunal accuses Donald Tusk of a coup. Indian outlet Hindustan Times highlights a video of the confrontation and describes Anna Korwin-Piotrowska as a Tusk-backed court head. Polish outlet Notes from Poland highlights the physical confrontation with judges. Swedish Radio's Ekot highlights the government's attempt to remove the court's head and the thousands of demonstrators outside the building.

Flávio Bolsonaro says from the US that he will run for president again
Flávio Bolsonaro is in the United States and says, according to El País, that he will again be a presidential candidate. According to El Tiempo, he left Brazil after a court order requiring him to hand over his passport, amid an investigation into alleged money laundering.

Actor Patrick Stewart dies at 86
Actor Patrick Stewart has died aged 86, according to several outlets, including Reuters and Deutsche Welle. He was known for playing Captain Jean-Luc Picard in Star Trek: The Next Generation and Professor Charles Xavier in the X-Men films.
How the world sees it: Sources from different countries highlight different parts of Stewart's career. Ars Technica (USA) and RNZ (New Zealand) publish personal tributes about his significance. The Hindu (India) and Daily Maverick (South Africa, with Reuters text) describe him as a Shakespearean stage actor who became a star of Star Trek and X-Men. Deutsche Welle (Germany) and Expressen (Sweden) highlight mainly Star Trek. NRK (Norway) mentions Star Trek and X-Men. El País América (Spain) highlights his career in pictures. Aftonbladet (Sweden) highlights tributes from fellow actors. The National (United Arab Emirates, state-controlled media) highlights Star Trek and X-Men.
