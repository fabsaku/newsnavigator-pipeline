Abdul El-Sayed wins Michigan Senate race, becoming first Muslim elected to US Senate

Published without manual review, source-checked.

Democrat Abdul El-Sayed won Michigan's Senate race over Republican Mike Rogers, according to media projections cited by several outlets, and becomes the first Muslim elected to the US Senate. A Reuters report describes attacks during the campaign, including from President Trump.

How the world sees it 5 regions
The headlines highlight different things. The Straits Times (Singapore) and Al-Monitor (US) highlight that 'politics of fear' failed to stop the win. Hindustan Times (India) highlights that El-Sayed defeated the Trump-backed Mike Rogers. Axios (US) highlights that a progressive candidate won in a battleground state. El Tiempo (Colombia) asks in its headline what lies behind the new faces in Congress and mentions earlier 'firsts' in the Senate. The National (UAE, state-controlled media) highlights that El-Sayed becomes the first Muslim senator.

Sources differ: The sources agree that El-Sayed won and becomes the first Muslim senator. They differ in how the win is linked to the course of the campaign: the Reuters text (via the Straits Times and Al-Monitor) describes attacks from President Trump and the Republican opponent, and campaign mailers with claims about terrorist ties and sharia law. Axios describes the race as an example of how an openly progressive candidate can win in a battleground state.

Sources: The Straits Times, Al-Monitor, El Tiempo, Dawn, The Hindu, and 4 more
