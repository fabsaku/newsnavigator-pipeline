Trump threatens to block or tax US firms building AI data centres abroad

21 November 2026 at 01:07 UTC. Published without manual review, machine source-checked.

Trump said on Friday that US companies building AI data centres overseas could be blocked or face a very high tax. According to the BBC, he gave few details about the proposal, and others in his team reportedly heard about it for the first time.

How the world sees it 6 regions
The Spanish outlet El País highlights that the president encouraged local communities to accept data centres despite growing opposition to the facilities. The Hong Kong-based South China Morning Post highlights a divide between the president's view that expansion is critical to the AI race and a US public that is sceptical of its benefits.

Sources differ: The sources describe the measure differently. Axios, the BBC and The New York Times write about companies being blocked or banned, while CNBC, The Verge, the Financial Times and Hindustan Times write about a tax. The Guardian, The Wall Street Journal and Dagens industri write about penalties. According to El País, Trump did not explain how the measure would be applied.
