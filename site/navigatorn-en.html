Democrats take control of US Congress in midterm elections

Democrats took control of both the House of Representatives and the Senate in the US midterm elections, according to several sources. The Wall Street Journal reports that the party also won a majority of governorships.

How the world sees it 7 regions
American sources mainly focus on what the result means for Trump and Congress, for example Fox News on Senate Republicans and The New York Times on Congress's ability to check the president. CGTN (state-controlled, China) highlights voters rejecting Republicans in historically red states, and The National (state-controlled, United Arab Emirates) highlights the Democrats' 'blue tsunami' and the setback for Trump. Focus Taiwan (Taiwan) highlights that Taiwan continues to enjoy support in the US Congress. Dawn (Pakistan) describes the result as a nationwide rejection of Trump.

US: blue and red AllSides
Media rated left or lean left by AllSides: Vox (AllSides: left) has a headline highlighting how Democrats won the Deep South, a regional angle. The New York Times (AllSides: lean left) has a headline highlighting that Congress is set to reassert power to check Trump. Media rated right by AllSides: Fox News (AllSides: right) has a headline saying Senate Republicans will not stand idly by, and its ingress quotes Senate Majority Leader John Thune on 'Democrat extremism'. Media rated center by AllSides: The Hill (AllSides: center) has a headline on demographic shifts behind the Democrats' victory, and The Wall Street Journal (AllSides: center) describes the result as a broad Democratic win in Congress and in governorships.

Sources differ: The sources differ in how they describe the causes of the result and Trump's reaction. Daily Maverick (South Africa) writes that voters punished Trump over economic pressures, rising fuel costs and the Iran war. Africanews writes that Trump admitted the Iran war may have played a part, while Buenos Aires Times writes that Trump tries to brush off the defeat. Fox News reports that Senate Republicans led by John Thune will not stand idly by, while The Straits Times writes that Trump said he would work with Democrats.

Sources: Hindustan Times, The Straits Times, Buenos Aires Times, Al Jazeera, The Hill, and 21 more

Vance says Iran war was behind Republican midterm losses

Vice President JD Vance says the war against Iran was the reason Republicans lost the midterm elections, according to several sources. Israel's UN ambassador Danny Danon rejects the claim, while Trump, according to SVT, says the war was worth it.

How the world sees it 7 regions
State-controlled media: Tehran Times (Iran) headlines that Americans punish Trump over the war on Iran and writes that voters handed Democrats control of Congress. The National (UAE) reports that oil rebounded from a two-month low while uncertainty over the war lingers. Hindustan Times (India) highlights Iranian officials arguing that the Republican defeat shows the war has cost the US politically and economically. The Straits Times (Singapore) writes that the next eight weeks are the optimal window for a US military strike on Iran. Meduza (Latvia) headlines Zelensky's hope for more US support from a Democratic Congress and says Moscow counts on Trump to keep up peace efforts. The sources thus highlight different aspects: Iranian and state-controlled reporting on the cost of the war, Meduza on Ukraine and Russia, and the other sources mainly on Vance's remarks.

US: blue and red AllSides
Media that AllSides rates as leaning left: NPR headlines that Senator Kirsten Gillibrand says Democrats must end the Iran war and hold Trump accountable, and its standfirst covers her priorities and the question of how they will be delivered. The New York Times headlines that the midterm losses complicate Trump's options on the Iran war. Axios headlines that oil prices fell sharply after a post by Vance and frames the election as pressure on the White House to find a way out of the conflict. Media that AllSides rates as leaning right: Reason headlines that J.D. Vance blames Iran for the Republican midterm losses. The left-rated sources highlight the Democratic agenda, the consequences for Trump's options and the oil market, while the right-rated source places Vance's own statement at the center.

Sources differ: According to Reuters (via Daily Maverick) and The Hindu, Vance says the Iran war was the reason for the losses, and Reuters writes that he blames it entirely. According to Middle East Eye, Israel's UN ambassador Danny Danon rejects the claim and says it is easy to always blame Israel. According to SVT Nyheter, Trump says the war was worth it. According to Hindustan Times, Iranian officials use the defeat to argue that the war has cost the US politically and economically.

Sources: Meduza, The Hill, Middle East Eye, NPR, The Straits Times, and 13 more

Protests across France during nationwide strike over cost of living

According to the French interior ministry, about 600,000 people joined protests across France on 5 November, while unions say the figure was far higher. The protests focused on cost of living and public services, and Dawn reports clashes with police in Paris and Lyon.

How the world sees it 7 regions
The headlines differ in what they highlight. India's The Hindu highlights the demand for a dignified wage, while Pakistan's Dawn highlights that the marches were largely peaceful but that some protesters clashed with police. Britain's BBC World and Germany's Deutsche Welle highlight rising participation numbers and growing anger over the cost of living. The UAE's The National, which is identified as a state-controlled outlet, highlights Macron's efforts to prevent civil unrest. Sources differ: Reported participation figures differ. The French interior ministry estimates about 600,000, according to BBC World, Deutsche Welle and France 24.
