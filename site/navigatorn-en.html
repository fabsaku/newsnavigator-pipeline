Houthis claim six attacks on Saudi airports and oil facilities

The Houthis claim, according to spokesperson Yahya Saree, six attacks on airports, oil facilities and a military headquarters in Saudi Arabia. Saree said ballistic missiles were fired at the Ras Tanura and Rabigh refineries.

How the world sees it 2 regions
Al Jazeera (Qatar) highlights the attacks on airports and oil facilities and talks between Iran and Mecca pact countries in Tehran earlier this week. Middle East Eye (UK) highlights spokesperson Yahya Saree and the claim that ballistic missiles were fired at the Ras Tanura and Rabigh refineries.

Sources: Sveriges Radio Ekot, Africanews

Spain train crash leaves at least 69 dead near Toledo

At least 69 people have died after a high-speed train hit the back of a stationary train near Toledo, Spain, on Wednesday, according to several outlets. Spain has begun three days of mourning, and search and rescue work in the wreckage continues.

How the world sees it 8 regions
The headlines differ in what they highlight. Spanish sources (El País and El País América) highlight recordings of communication with the rail control center, where according to El País América the driver had warned that the train could not move, and criticism from the rail industry. British and French sources (BBC, The Guardian, France 24) highlight victims, grief, the search work and expert assessments of the causes. Chinese state-controlled media (CGTN) highlight, according to a first preliminary report, an estimated speed of 160 to 200 km/h at impact. Iranian state-controlled media (Tehran Times) highlight the death toll and state that, according to authorities, the crash is one of Europe's deadliest of this kind this century.

Sources differ: The death toll differs: The Hindu and Tehran Times report 68, while others, including Le Monde, The Guardian and Sveriges Radio Ekot, report 69. The assessments of the cause also differ. Railway researcher Oskar Fröidh tells SVT that a human mistake was decisive for the crash. Expert Francisco Badea challenges explanations in France 24 that reduce the crash to isolated human mistakes. The New York Times writes that Spain is asking whether its railway system is too old and prone to human error. El País reports that the rail industry asks that no one be blamed until the causes are known and points to a lack of investment and staff.

Sources: The Guardian, Al Jazeera, CNA, El Tiempo, The Hindu, and 10 more

Christine Lagarde to step down as ECB president early, effective 7 February 2027

Published without manual review, source-checked.

The European Central Bank (ECB) announced on Thursday that Christine Lagarde will leave her post as president on 7 February 2027, almost nine months before her term ends in October. According to European Council President Antonio Costa, the successor search has begun.

How the world sees it 7 regions
Some Asian sources (South China Morning Post, Japan Times and Rappler) highlight the economic and political challenges facing Europe, including inflation risks, rising bond yields and the election campaign in France. The Straits Times instead highlights Lagarde's career and her way of leading the ECB. The Hindu highlights the resignation itself. European and Latin American sources (Euractiv, Deutsche Welle, Sveriges Radio Ekot, SVT and El País) mainly highlight the announcement, its timing and the succession question. The state-controlled outlet The National, from the United Arab Emirates, headlines only that Lagarde resigns after months of speculation.

Sources differ: Sources differ on the reason for the early exit, or give none. France 24 reports that Lagarde wants more freedom to engage in matters outside monetary policy. Rappler, citing an anonymous source with direct knowledge, reports that she resigned to weigh in on political matters in France and globally. El País writes that Lagarde does not clarify her future plans, while France 24 says the move will fuel speculation about them.

Sources: Europeiska centralbanken, El País América, France 24, Euractiv, MercoPress, and 8 more
