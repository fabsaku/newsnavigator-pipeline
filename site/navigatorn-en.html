All 69 victims of Spanish train crash identified

Published without manual review, source-checked.

Spanish authorities have identified all 69 people killed in a high-speed train collision in Urda, central Spain, on 28 October, according to several outlets. Two Japanese nationals are among the dead, according to The Straits Times and The Japan Times.

How the world sees it 6 regions
Sources from different countries highlight different aspects of the event. Japanese and Singaporean outlets highlight that two Japanese nationals are among the dead. Mexico News Daily highlights a Mexican-born man living in Oklahoma City who was among the victims. Chinese state media (CGTN) highlight that 29 injured people remain hospitalised. NRK highlights the number of foreign nationals among those identified.

Sources differ: The sources differ in timing and in their figures on identification. NRK (2 Nov.) reported that 66 of 69 had been identified, 12 of them foreigners. SVT (1 Nov.), citing El Mundo, wrote that twelve people were still missing. The Straits Times (2 Nov.) reported that all 69 had been identified, with 54 Spanish nationals and 15 foreigners.

Sources: The Hindu, ABC News (Australien), The Straits Times, BBC World, Hindustan Times, and 12 more

Russia bombards Ukraine's power grid ahead of Abu Dhabi peace talks

Published without manual review, source-checked.

Russia struck Ukraine's energy infrastructure with missiles and drones, according to Reuters, a week before US-brokered talks. Witkoff says the US, Russia and Ukraine will hold talks in Abu Dhabi on 10 and 11 November.

How the world sees it 5 regions
The headlines highlight different parts of the news. Russian state media (RT) highlights Witkoff announcing the next round of negotiations. Emirati state media (The National) highlights the US saying the Abu Dhabi talks will take place this month. Chinese state media (CGTN) highlights the US bringing together delegations from Ukraine and Russia to discuss ending the conflict, post-conflict security agreements and de-escalation. The Kyiv Independent highlights the European Commission hosting talks on Ukraine in Brussels on 4 November, and Euractiv highlights talks between the US, Europe and NATO in Brussels. The Japan Times and Al-Monitor highlight Russia's attacks on Ukraine's power grid and Merz urging Moscow to engage.

US: blue and red AllSides
Media that AllSides rates as leaning left (Axios) and as right (Fox News) highlight different things. The Axios headline says the US, Russia and Ukraine will resume peace talks, and its summary notes that diplomacy was suspended in February due to the war in Iran. Axios cites US officials saying the meeting results from talks between Trump's envoys. The Fox News headline says the US will mediate upcoming talks between Ukraine and Russia, and its summary says the talks focus on security arrangements and de-escalation. Fox News reports Witkoff's announcement.

Sources differ: The sources describe the participants in the Abu Dhabi talks differently. The Hindu, which reports that the Kremlin confirms the talks, refers to talks between Ukraine and the US. Axios and RT refer to talks between the US, Russia and Ukraine. Yonhap says Melania Trump will meet representatives this week, while Meduza gives no date for the meeting at the White House.

Sources: The Japan Times, CNBC, Al-Monitor, The Straits Times, Yonhap, and 11 more
