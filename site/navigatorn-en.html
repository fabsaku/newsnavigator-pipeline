Russia bombards Ukraine's power grid ahead of Abu Dhabi peace talks

Published without manual review, source-checked.

Russia struck Ukraine's energy infrastructure with missiles and drones, according to Reuters, a week before US-brokered talks. Witkoff says the US, Russia and Ukraine will hold talks in Abu Dhabi on 10 and 11 November.

How the world sees it 3 regions
The headlines highlight different parts of the news. Russian state media (RT) highlights Witkoff announcing the next round of negotiations. Emirati state media (The National) highlights the US saying the Abu Dhabi talks will take place this month. Chinese state media (CGTN) highlights the US bringing together delegations from Ukraine and Russia to discuss ending the conflict, post-conflict security agreements and de-escalation. The Kyiv Independent highlights the European Commission hosting talks on Ukraine in Brussels on 4 November, and Euractiv highlights talks between the US, Europe and NATO in Brussels. The Japan Times and Al-Monitor highlight Russia's attacks on Ukraine's power grid and Merz urging Moscow to engage.

Sources differ: The sources describe the participants in the Abu Dhabi talks differently. The Hindu, which reports that the Kremlin confirms the talks, refers to talks between Ukraine and the US. Axios and RT refer to talks between the US, Russia and Ukraine. Yonhap says Melania Trump will meet representatives this week, while Meduza gives no date for the meeting at the White House.

Sources: Al-Monitor, Euractiv, The Hindu, Meduza, CGTN (state-controlled)

Canada says Russian and Belarusian players will not compete at world junior championship

Published without manual review, source-checked.

Canadian Prime Minister Mark Carney says Russian and Belarusian players will not be able to take part in the world junior championship in Canada. Several European countries, including Sweden and Finland, have reportedly said they will not play Russian teams.

How the world sees it 2 regions
The headlines highlight different parts of the story. Kyiv Independent (Ukraine) highlights that Canada will use immigration law to keep Russian and Belarusian athletes from competing. The Guardian (UK) highlights that Canada has joined a coalition of countries refusing to play Russian teams. Hindustan Times (India) highlights the prime minister's announcement and several countries threatening a boycott. RT, a state-controlled Russian media outlet, highlights that four Nordic countries refuse to play Russia and that Canada opposes Russia's return.

Sources differ: The sources differ on which tournament is affected. Kyiv Independent, SVT and Hindustan Times report on the world junior championship, while Yle and Dagens Nyheter write about the world championship, which Yle says is scheduled for Germany in May. Hindustan Times describes several countries threatening a boycott, while Yle says Finland refuses to play Russia. Meduza says it is unclear how the CAS ruling will be carried out in practice.

Sources: The Guardian, Yle News, RT (state-controlled)
