Judge halts construction of Trump's arch in Washington for 14 days

A federal judge has ordered a 14-day pause on construction of Trump's triumphal arch in Washington. Several sources report that a federal planning commission had approved the project hours earlier, with an 8-4 vote according to Deutsche Welle.
