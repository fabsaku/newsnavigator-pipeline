Around 30 killed in attack on market in Taiz, Yemen

Around 30 people were killed in an attack on a market in Taiz province, Yemen, according to several sources. The Houthis say Saudi aircraft carried out the strikes. Yemen's internationally recognised government and the Houthis blame each other for the attack.
