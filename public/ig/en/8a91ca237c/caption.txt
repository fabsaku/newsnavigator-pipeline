Bank of England raises interest rates to 4%, first increase in over three years

The Bank of England raised its benchmark rate to 4%, according to The Guardian. Dawn reports the move was a quarter-point rise, the first since 2023, aimed at fighting inflation driven up by soaring energy costs.

Sources differ: The Wall Street Journal uses a different reference point in its headline: the first increase since the start of the Iran war.
