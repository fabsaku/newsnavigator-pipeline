All 69 victims of Spanish train crash identified

Spanish authorities have identified all 69 people killed in a high-speed train collision in Urda, central Spain, on 28 October, according to several outlets. Two Japanese nationals are among the dead, according to The Straits Times and The Japan Times.

How the world sees it: Sources from different countries highlight different aspects of the event. Japanese and Singaporean outlets highlight that two Japanese nationals are among the dead. Mexico News Daily highlights a Mexican-born man living in Oklahoma City who was among the victims. Chinese state media (CGTN) highlight that 29 injured people remain hospitalised. NRK highlights the number of foreign nationals among those identified.

Sources differ: The sources differ in timing and in their figures on identification. NRK (2 Nov.) reported that 66 of 69 had been identified, 12 of them foreigners. SVT (1 Nov.), citing El Mundo, wrote that twelve people were still missing. The Straits Times (2 Nov.) reported that all 69 had been identified, with 54 Spanish nationals and 15 foreigners.

Sources: The Japan Times, The Straits Times, Mexico News Daily, NRK, SVT Nyheter, Le Monde (English), CGTN (state-controlled)
