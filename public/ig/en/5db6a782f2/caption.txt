Paraguay's President Santiago Peña resigns after bank collapse

Paraguay's President Santiago Peña has resigned after the collapse of Ueno Bank, according to several sources, which report that millions of customer accounts have been frozen. According to NPR and the BBC, lawmakers have voted to pursue impeachment proceedings against him.

The sources differ on timing and on how the impeachment is described.
