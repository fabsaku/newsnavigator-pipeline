Trump wants nuclear arms cuts on the agenda at summit with Xi and Putin

Trump, Xi Jinping and Vladimir Putin are set to hold their first trilateral meeting on the sidelines of APEC, according to DW. Defense News reports that Trump wants to discuss cutting nuclear weapons, but that prospects are low.

NPR reports that Trump says he plans to discuss Ukraine during his Asia trip, which contradicts what the White House previously announced.
