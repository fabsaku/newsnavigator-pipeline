Canada says Russian and Belarusian players will not compete at world junior championship

Canadian Prime Minister Mark Carney says Russian and Belarusian players will not be able to take part in the world junior championship in Canada. Several European countries, including Sweden and Finland, have reportedly said they will not play Russian teams.

The sources differ on which tournament is affected.
