Iran says ships caught fire in the Strait of Hormuz

Iran says five oil tankers and a tugboat caught fire in the Strait of Hormuz, according to statements from the Revolutionary Guards and the army. Al-Monitor, citing Reuters, reports that three tankers were damaged.

The sources differ on the number of vessels.
