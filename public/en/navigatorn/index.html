Around 30 killed in attack on market in Taiz, Yemen

6 November 2026 at 23:09 UTC. Published without manual review, machine source-checked.

Around 30 people were killed in an attack on a market in Taiz province, Yemen, according to several sources. The Houthis say Saudi aircraft carried out the strikes. Yemen's internationally recognised government and the Houthis blame each other for the attack.

How the world sees it 4 regions
The headlines highlight different things. Swedish Ekot reports the attack and death toll without naming a perpetrator in the headline. Qatari Al Jazeera and British Middle East Eye highlight in their headlines that the government and the Houthis blame each other. Indian The Hindu highlights the airstrikes and attributes the claim to the Houthis.

Sources differ: According to Al Jazeera and Middle East Eye, Yemen's government and the Houthis blame each other for the attack. The Houthis say Saudi aircraft carried out the airstrikes. The material does not include the government's own account of what happened.

Sources: Middle East Eye, Al Jazeera, BBC World, The Hindu, Deutsche Welle, and 3 more

Democrats win US midterm elections

7 November 2026 at 10:10 UTC. Published without manual review, machine source-checked.

Democrats won the US midterm elections, according to CGTN and Le Monde. CGTN says public anger over living costs, immigration crackdowns and the Iran war contributed to the result, while Al Jazeera writes that the Iran war helped Democrats win.

How the world sees it 4 regions
Al Jazeera (Qatar) and CGTN (Chinese state media) highlight the Iran war as an explanation for the outcome. CGTN also highlights living costs and immigration policy, and that the new Congress may challenge Trump's domestic agenda and launch investigations. Le Monde (France) links the election to Lula's win in Brazil and to the limits of the far right in the Americas. Vox (US) focuses on what the election could mean for Trump's Cabinet.

Sources: Al Jazeera, Vox, Le Monde (English) (paywall), CGTN (state-controlled)

Democrats win big in US midterm elections

7 November 2026 at 11:15 UTC.

Several sources report that Democrats won by a wide margin in the US midterm elections. Fox News reports that James Talarico beat Ken Paxton in Texas by more than twelve percentage points.

How the world sees it 2 regions
The Indian source Hindustan Times highlights moderate Democrats as the driving force and progressive wins. The US sources instead highlight the size of the win (Politico and Fox News), voter turnout (The Washington Post), and that climate change received little attention in the campaigns (Axios).

US: blue and red AllSides
Outlets rated by AllSides as leaning left (Politico, Axios, and The Washington Post) highlight in their headlines that the Democratic gains were larger than expected according to Politico, that climate change was barely mentioned according to Axios, and that voter turnout was high according to The Washington Post. Politico quotes the president of House Majority PAC. The outlet rated by AllSides as right (Fox News) highlights in its headline that Democrats in Texas were stunned and in its lead that Talarico beat Paxton by more than twelve percentage points. Fox News quotes Texas Democrats.

Sources differ: Hindustan Times writes that moderate Democrats were the driving force behind the wave and that progressives mostly had easy wins. The Washington Post instead highlights high voter turnout as the driver. The sources thus give different explanations for what drove the result.

Sources: Hindustan Times, Politico, Fox News, Axios, The Washington Post (paywall)

Russia Has Gained 46 Square Miles in Ten Months

7 November 2026 at 20:09 UTC. Published without manual review, machine source-checked.

The Wall Street Journal and Hindustan Times report that Russia has gained 46 square miles over ten months. Hindustan Times says Ukrainian forces have foiled a Russian offensive in Donetsk, and the Russian army is making one more push.

How the world sees it 2 regions
The Indian outlet Hindustan Times and the American Wall Street Journal share the same headline, which highlights Russian failure on the battlefield and the 46 square miles. The American New York Times instead highlights an operation that helped Ukraine reverse some Russian gains.

Sources differ: The New York Times headline is about an operation that, according to the headline, helped Ukraine reverse some Russian gains. Hindustan Times and The Wall Street Journal instead highlight that Russia has gained 46 square miles and, according to the headline, is failing on the battlefield.

Sources: Hindustan Times, The New York Times (paywall), The Wall Street Journal (paywall)

Melania Trump meets Russian and Ukrainian representatives at White House on Oleshky

6 November 2026 at 23:09 UTC. Published without manual review, machine source-checked.

According to Meduza, Melania Trump met Russian and Ukrainian representatives at the White House and received Ukraine's Order of Princess Olha. According to the Washington Examiner, a short ceasefire in Oleshky allowed hundreds of civilians to be evacuated.

How the world sees it 4 regions
The headlines highlight different parts of the event. The Washington Examiner highlights the Ukrainian government's optimism about further localized ceasefires. Fox News highlights Melania Trump's description of the ceasefire as a possible step toward progress. Meduza highlights the award presented to Melania Trump. Hindustan Times highlights that an envoy of Putin takes part in the meeting. RT, a state-controlled Russian medium, highlights that Melania Trump is hosting Russian and Ukrainian envoys.

Sources differ: The sources describe Melania Trump's role differently. Hindustan Times states that she brokered the ceasefire in Oleshky, while the Washington Examiner reports that the Ukrainian government hopes she may help broker additional localized ceasefires. Fox News and RT do not say who brokered the ceasefire.

Sources: The Kyiv Independent, France 24, El Tiempo, Sveriges Radio Ekot, Hindustan Times, and 3 more
