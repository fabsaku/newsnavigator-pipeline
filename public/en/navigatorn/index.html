Trump offers UFC chief Dana White White House press secretary job: White's response unclear

8 November 2026 at 02:08 UTC. Published without manual review, machine source-checked.

Donald Trump says he has offered UFC CEO Dana White the job of White House press secretary and urges criminal probes of Democrats, according to several outlets. The White House has had no press secretary since October 16.

The sources highlight different parts. US outlets highlight the offer and Trump's own post. Japan Times (Japan) and CNA (Singapore) highlight Trump's call for criminal probes of Democrats. Dawn (Pakistan) and The Straits Times (Singapore) highlight Trump's request that White bring some fighters along as assistants. South China Morning Post (Hong Kong) highlights Trump's combative tone ahead of an incoming Democratic Congress. Hindustan Times (India) and NRK (Norway) highlight that White declined. State-controlled Russian RT highlights the offer and White's longtime friendship with Trump.
