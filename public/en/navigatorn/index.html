Democrats take control of US Congress in midterm elections

Published without manual review, source-checked.

Several sources report that Democrats have taken control of both the House of Representatives and the Senate in the US midterm elections. The sources link the result to Trump's policies, including the war in Iran and economic issues such as fuel prices.

How the world sees it 5 regions
The headlines highlight different parts of the election. Hindustan Times (India) highlights Kamala Harris's statement on a possible 2028 presidential run. The Japan Times (Japan) and Al-Monitor (USA) highlight Trump's choice between a deal and escalation with Iran. Buenos Aires Times (Argentina) highlights what the result may mean for Argentina. CNA (Singapore) highlights Trump's China diplomacy, and Deutsche Welle (Germany) highlights economic issues such as oil prices, tariffs and mounting debt. The National, a state-controlled medium in the UAE, highlights that Brent oil fell on uncertainty about Iran. Tehran Times, state-controlled media in Iran, highlights that a strong Democratic performance would not necessarily prevent a tough Iran policy. CGTN, state-controlled media in China, highlights the tensions between the US and Iran.

Sources differ: The sources agree that Democrats gained a majority in the House of Representatives. The Hindu mentions only the House in its headline, while Daily Maverick, El País and CNA also mention the Senate. On what the result means for Iran policy, the interpretations differ: Tehran Times writes that a strong Democratic performance would not necessarily prevent Trump from a tough line toward Iran, while The Japan Times and Al-Monitor describe Trump as facing a choice between a deal and escalation.

Sources: Hindustan Times, The Japan Times, Al Jazeera, CNA, South China Morning Post, and 13 more
