Trump says Putin wants to end Ukraine war

20 November 2026 at 03:07 UTC. Published without manual review, machine source-checked.

According to Axios and Meduza, Trump told European leaders from Air Force One that Putin wants to end the war in Ukraine. After a meeting with Xi Jinping and Putin at the APEC summit, Trump says the leaders also discussed the war in Iran.

The headlines highlight different parts of the events. Australian ABC reports that Trump said Europe could save money by ending the war. South African Daily Maverick reports that Trump urged Xi that the US and China can help end the war. French France 24 highlights that the war in Ukraine continues. British Middle East Eye reports Trump's statement that Xi and Putin want the war in Iran to end. American Wall Street Journal and New York Times highlight that the three leaders condemned nuclear war.

Sources differ: Trump says Putin wants to end the war. Meduza reports that Putin said Moscow will not negotiate with Kyiv after strikes on the Crimean Bridge.

Merz: European leaders could join new Ukraine talks soon

20 November 2026 at 09:05 UTC. Published without manual review, machine source-checked.

German Chancellor Friedrich Merz says European leaders could join new Ukraine talks soon. The United Arab Emirates is named as a possible location, according to The Straits Times.

How the world sees it 3 regions
German (Deutsche Welle), Singaporean (The Straits Times) and Indian (Hindustan Times) sources mainly highlight Merz's statement that Europe is expected to join new talks. British BBC highlights that European allies say they have a united negotiating position and are ready for talks with Russia. The National, from the United Arab Emirates and identified in the source material as a state-controlled medium, highlights that the US pledges cooperation with Europe on the talks.

Sources differ: The sources differ in the details they highlight. The Straits Times and Hindustan Times report that Merz expects Europe to join soon, while Deutsche Welle refers to "neutral ground" without naming a location. The United Arab Emirates is named only by The Straits Times.

Sources: Deutsche Welle, BBC World, The Straits Times, Hindustan Times, The National (state-controlled)

European countries expect to join new Ukraine talks, says Merz

20 November 2026 at 01:10 UTC. Published without manual review, machine source-checked.

German Chancellor Friedrich Merz says European leaders could join new Ukraine talks soon, according to Deutsche Welle. At a meeting in Berlin, European allies of Ukraine say they have a united negotiating position, BBC reports.

How the world sees it 3 regions
British BBC highlights European unity ahead of talks with Russia. German Deutsche Welle highlights Merz's statement that European leaders could take part on neutral ground, and that the UK and EU countries want a seat at the table. Singapore's The Straits Times and India's Hindustan Times highlight that Europe expects to join talks soon, and The Straits Times names the United Arab Emirates as a possible location. Colombian El Tiempo highlights in its headline a French proposal on Ukraine's path to NATO and a German call for peace in Europe. The UAE's The National, a state-controlled medium, highlights a US pledge of cooperation with Europe on Ukraine-Russia talks.

Sources: BBC World, Politico Europe, Meduza, The Hindu, France 24, and 2 more
