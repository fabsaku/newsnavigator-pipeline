Around 30 killed in attack on market in Taiz, Yemen

6 November 2026 at 23:09 UTC. Published without manual review, machine source-checked.

Around 30 people were killed in an attack on a market in Taiz province, Yemen, according to several sources. The Houthis say Saudi aircraft carried out the strikes. Yemen's internationally recognised government and the Houthis blame each other for the attack.

How the world sees it 4 regions
The headlines highlight different things. Swedish Ekot reports the attack and death toll without naming a perpetrator in the headline. Qatari Al Jazeera and British Middle East Eye highlight in their headlines that the government and the Houthis blame each other. Indian The Hindu highlights the airstrikes and attributes the claim to the Houthis.

Sources differ: According to Al Jazeera and Middle East Eye, Yemen's government and the Houthis blame each other for the attack. The Houthis say Saudi aircraft carried out the airstrikes. The material does not include the government's own account of what happened.

Sources: Middle East Eye, France 24, Sveriges Radio Ekot, The Hindu
