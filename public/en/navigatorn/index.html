Trump says he may meet Kim Jong Un in China

17 November 2026 at 12:08 UTC. Published without manual review, machine source-checked.

Trump says he may meet North Korean leader Kim Jong Un in China, according to Yonhap and The Straits Times. Arab News reports that China says it has no information about such a meeting.
