EU and Canada deepen cooperation and aim for associate membership

Published without manual review, source-checked.

The EU and Canada are deepening cooperation in several areas, the parties announced at a summit in Montreal. According to Euractiv, they will define their future partnership by 2027.

How the world sees it 2 regions
SVT highlights the goal of associate membership for Canada, according to Carney. Euractiv highlights the 2027 timeframe and that political hurdles remain. The Wall Street Journal highlights the path toward the arrangement.

Sources differ: SVT writes that the goal is for Canada to become the EU's first associate member, according to Prime Minister Mark Carney. The Wall Street Journal writes that the leaders have set out a path toward an associate membership arrangement. Euractiv writes that Canada is moving closer to associate membership but that political hurdles remain.

Sources: SVT Nyheter, The Wall Street Journal (paywall)

Democrats hope to win back Congress in US midterm elections

Published without manual review, source-checked.

Midterm elections in the US are held on Tuesday, with all 435 House seats and 35 of 100 Senate seats at stake. According to MercoPress, Democrats hope to regain control of Congress, and the vote is described as a test of Donald Trump's second presidency.

How the world sees it 4 regions
South African Daily Maverick highlights factors such as early voter turnout, slumping approval ratings, inflation and an ongoing war, and reports that Republicans are countering with support from well-funded backers and efforts to impose voter suppression tactics. Uruguayan MercoPress highlights the Democrats' hopes and polls showing them in a favourable position. Colombian El Tiempo focuses on Latino voters and reports that Democrats lead by about 30 points among Hispanics, a group the paper describes as decisive in nearly two dozen competitive districts. Sweden's Sveriges Radio Ekot focuses on the city of Youngstown, Ohio, presented as a symbol of the campaign, and notes that not everyone in the city welcomes the attention. Swedish business daily Dagens industri has a headline calling one issue the most important of the US election, but the headline does not name the issue. British Financial Times headlines that bond investors are staying out ahead of the vote.

Sources: Daily Maverick, MercoPress, Sveriges Radio Ekot, El Tiempo, Dagens industri (paywall), and 1 more
