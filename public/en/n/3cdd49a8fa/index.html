Democrats win big in US midterm elections

Several sources report that Democrats won by a wide margin in the US midterm elections. Fox News reports that James Talarico beat Ken Paxton in Texas by more than twelve percentage points.

How the world sees it

The Indian source Hindustan Times highlights moderate Democrats as the driving force and progressive wins. The US sources instead highlight the size of the win (Politico and Fox News), voter turnout (The Washington Post), and that climate change received little attention in the campaigns (Axios).

Sources differ

Hindustan Times writes that moderate Democrats were the driving force behind the wave and that progressives mostly had easy wins. The Washington Post instead highlights high voter turnout as the driver. The sources thus give different explanations for what drove the result.
