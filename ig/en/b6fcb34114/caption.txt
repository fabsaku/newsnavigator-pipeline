Tankers catch fire in Strait of Hormuz, Iran says

Iran says tankers caught fire in the Strait of Hormuz after hitting sea mines. The Iranian navy says one vessel entered a restricted zone despite repeated warnings.

The sources differ on the number of tankers: The Hindu and Dagens industri report two, while Tehran Times, citing the Iranian navy, reports one.

Sources: The Hindu, ABC News (Australien), Dagens industri, Tehran Times (state-controlled)
