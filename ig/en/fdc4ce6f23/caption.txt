US introduces rules limiting Chinese firms' remote access to AI chips

Tom's Hardware reports new US rules aimed at blocking Chinese firms' remote access to AI chips. CNA reports that data centres anywhere need a US licence to supply large computing volumes to firms headquartered in China and other countries, and China criticises the move.

The sources highlight different effects: BBC World writes that the new rules will hit China, while Hindustan Times points to risks for Nvidia and Apple.
